> **"Building builders, not just learners."**

Technology is changing faster than ever, and cloud computing has become the backbone of today's digital world. From global companies to ambitious startups, organisations everywhere depend on the cloud to build applications, manage data, and deliver smart, scalable solutions. Recognising how important these technologies have become, the **AWS Student Builder Group (SBG)** at **Graphic Era Deemed to be University** was established in **2025** with a simple vision—to help students move beyond textbooks by learning cloud computing through hands-on experience, innovation, and real-world problem-solving.

The AWS Student Builder Group is more than just a technical community; it's a place where students learn by building. Instead of focusing only on theory, the group encourages members to explore new ideas, work on practical projects, collaborate with like-minded peers, and develop skills that matter in the industry. It provides an environment where curiosity is encouraged, mistakes become learning opportunities, and every project helps members grow as engineers.

Students in the community explore a wide range of technologies, including **Amazon Web Services (AWS), Artificial Intelligence, Full-Stack Development, DevOps,** and **Open Source**. Whether someone is taking their first step into cloud computing or already working on advanced projects, SBG offers opportunities to learn, contribute, and continuously improve.

In a short span of time, the AWS Student Builder Group has grown into one of the fastest-growing technical communities at Graphic Era University. Through hands-on workshops, certification drives, hackathons, project-based learning, technical talks, and community events, the group has created a space where students don't just learn technology—they build with it. By connecting passionate learners, experienced mentors, and industry-relevant technologies, SBG continues to inspire students to transform ideas into meaningful solutions and prepare themselves for the future of technology.

## Our Vision

To create a vibrant community of builders where every student has the opportunity to learn, innovate, collaborate, and grow using modern cloud technologies while developing the technical and leadership skills needed to solve real-world challenges.

## Our Mission

At AWS Student Builder Group GEU, our goal is to help students become confident cloud professionals by giving them opportunities to learn, build, and grow in a supportive community. We believe the best way to learn technology is by working on real projects, collaborating with others, and continuously exploring new ideas.

Our mission is to:

- Encourage hands-on learning through real-world projects powered by AWS.
- Inspire innovation by exploring cloud computing, Artificial Intelligence, and modern software development.
- Help students prepare for and achieve AWS certifications with the right guidance and resources.
- Foster a culture of collaboration through peer learning, mentorship, and knowledge sharing.
- Connect students with industry through workshops, technical sessions, hackathons, and community events.
- Empower students to become lifelong learners and builders who can create scalable, impactful technology solutions for the future.

## Why AWS Student Builder Group GEU?

In the present-day engineering world, it is not enough to simply have theoretical knowledge. Graduates need to better figure out ways to architect cloud solutions, automate deployments, create scalable applications, and change with fast-growing technologies.

AWS Student Builder Group GEU responds to this challenge by creating opportunities for students to learn through designing. All activities aim to transform group members from passive students into builders who can deploy real applications, understand production environments, and contribute to team-based efforts.

The group believes that actual learning occurs through practice, which is why all seminars, challenges, and occasions are oriented on real experiences.

## Building a Culture of Innovation

The achievements of any technical community depend not just on technologies, but the spirit it embodies.

AWS Student Builder Group GEU boasts a stimulating environment where students share knowledge, exchange ideas, mentor fellow students, and engage in valuable projects. Members are encouraged to test things out, ask questions, take part in open-source initiatives, and constantly broaden their technical horizons.

This collaborative culture allows students from a wide range of backgrounds to pursue cloud computing in their own right.

## Cloud Ignite '26 — Where the Journey Began

Every community has a moment that marks the beginning of its journey.

For the **AWS Student Builder Group GEU**, that moment was **Cloud Ignite '26**—the event that officially introduced the chapter to the students of Graphic Era University.

More than just an inaugural event, Cloud Ignite '26 was designed to give students their first real experience with cloud computing. Instead of simply listening to presentations, participants explored AWS through hands-on activities, deployed their very first live website, and discovered how cloud technologies power the applications and services we use every day.

The response from students was overwhelming. The event received **over 350 registrations**, and due to venue limitations, participation was capped at **120+ students**. The gathering was graced by distinguished faculty members, including **Dr. Durga Prasad Gangodkar** and **Dr. Amit Kumar**, whose encouragement highlighted the importance of emerging cloud technologies in today's rapidly evolving tech landscape.

The student leadership team played a key role in making the event a success by organising technical sessions, live demonstrations, and interactive learning experiences that kept participants engaged throughout the day. For many attendees, it was their first opportunity to work with AWS and understand how cloud platforms are used to build, deploy, and scale real-world applications.

Cloud Ignite '26 was more than the launch of a student community—it was the start of a culture centred around building, experimenting, and learning together. It inspired students to take their first steps into cloud computing while opening doors to exciting career paths in **Cloud Engineering, DevOps,** and **Backend Development**. The event laid a strong foundation for the AWS Student Builder Group and set the stage for many more learning opportunities to come.

## AWS Builder Challenge 2026 — Transforming Learners into Builders

Building on the momentum of its successful launch, the **AWS Student Builder Group GEU** introduced one of its flagship initiatives—the **AWS Builder Challenge 2026**.

Designed as a **21-day cloud learning journey**, the challenge aimed to help students develop a solid understanding of cloud computing while preparing them for the **AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02)** certification. It wasn't just about completing lessons—it was about building consistency, gaining practical experience, and learning alongside a community of like-minded peers.

Throughout the three weeks, participants followed a structured learning path that combined theory with hands-on practice. Daily learning missions, guided exercises, mentorship sessions, mock tests, and interactive discussions kept students engaged as they progressed from cloud fundamentals to working with real AWS services. With continuous support from mentors and fellow participants, learning became a collaborative and rewarding experience.

The response to the challenge reflected the growing enthusiasm for cloud technologies within the university community. The initiative received **426 registrations**, with **161 students successfully completing** the entire program. Along the way, participants gained valuable hands-on AWS experience, strengthened their certification readiness.

More importantly, the AWS Builder Challenge was about much more than certification preparation. It encouraged students to stay consistent, think critically, solve real problems, and build the confidence to take on more advanced cloud technologies. For many participants, it marked the beginning of a long-term journey into the world of AWS and cloud engineering.

Behind every successful initiative is a dedicated team of passionate student leaders.

Working alongside faculty mentors, the leadership team ensures that every event reflects professionalism, technical excellence, and the collaborative spirit of the global AWS community.
